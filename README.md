🎯 Rehearse AI – AI-Powered Mock Interview Platform

An intelligent mock interview platform designed to help candidates prepare for real-world technical and behavioral interviews. Rehearse AI analyzes your resume, gathers relevant interview insights for your target company and role, and conducts an interactive voice-enabled mock interview with detailed feedback and performance analysis.

✨ Key Features

📄 PDF Resume Parser & Drag & Drop: Upload your PDF resume and instantly extract relevant information for a personalized interview experience.

🌐 Company & Role Research: Researches relevant interview questions, technologies, and company-specific insights based on your target role and organization.

🎙️ Voice-Enabled Mock Interview: Practice answering questions using speech-to-text while receiving realistic voice-based interview prompts.

📊 Detailed Evaluation & Feedback: Receive 0–10 scores along with analysis of your strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

💡 Sample Answers & Suggestions: Get example responses and actionable suggestions to improve your interview performance.

📈 Performance Report: Receive a comprehensive report summarizing your interview performance after completing a session.

🚀 How It Works

Upload Your Resume – Add your PDF resume to personalize the interview.

Choose Your Target – Select the company, role, and interview focus.

Research & Preparation – The platform gathers relevant information about the role and interview process.

Start the Interview – Answer personalized questions through an interactive voice-enabled experience.

Get Evaluated – Receive detailed scores, feedback, and improvement suggestions.

Review Your Report – Analyze your overall performance and identify areas to work on.
